A bit about me

Design is how I think.
AI is how I scale it.

I'm a UI designer with a product manager's brain — currently at Listen2it, where I've designed three AI products end to end. My work spans web and mobile, from dashboards and editing workspaces to marketing sites and 500+ AI-generated avatars.

Design started for me as a love of aesthetics and a curiosity about how people interact with technology. It became something more useful: the ability to stand between a complex AI system and a confused human, and make the two understand each other.

About — how I work

A process built for AI-speed products

01

Understand

Competitive teardowns, user workflows and the honest question: what does the model actually do well?

02

Define the fast path

Every product gets one obvious primary action. Depth and power-user tools unfold progressively.

03

Design with AI

Midjourney, Runway and agents are part of my toolchain — for assets, variants and testing ideas at volume.

04

Ship & refine

Close collaboration with engineering, real-user feedback, and relentless iteration after launch.

Toolkit: Figma · Adobe XD · Sketch · Framer · After Effects for craft · Midjourney · Veo 3 · Runway ML · ChatGPT · AI Agents for scale · strategy, roadmapping & agile from my product management side.
